# Marketing Decision Trees

Quick decision guides for common marketing situations. Use these when you need to choose between approaches.

---

## Traffic Acquisition Decision Tree

```
Need more traffic?
├── Need traffic NOW?
│   ├── Yes → Paid advertising
│   │   ├── Budget >$5k/month? → Google Ads + Social Ads
│   │   └── Budget <$5k/month? → Social Ads + Retargeting
│   └── No → Organic growth
│       ├── Have content? → SEO optimization
│       └── No content? → Content marketing
│           ├── B2B? → LinkedIn + Blog
│           └── B2C? → Social + Video
```

## Content Type Decision Tree

```
What content to create?
├── What stage of funnel?
│   ├── TOFU (Awareness)
│   │   ├── Educational? → Blog posts, guides
│   │   ├── Entertaining? → Social, video
│   │   └── Inspirational? → Stories, case studies
│   ├── MOFU (Consideration)
│   │   ├── Comparison? → Comparison pages, webinars
│   │   ├── How-to? → Tutorials, courses
│   │   └── Proof? → Case studies, testimonials
│   └── BOFU (Decision)
│       ├── Product info? → Product pages, demos
│       ├── Pricing? → Pricing pages, ROI calculators
│       └── Trust? → Reviews, guarantees
```

## Email Sequence Decision Tree

```
What email sequence to build?
├── What's the goal?
│   ├── Onboarding? → Welcome sequence (5-7 emails)
│   ├── Nurture? → Lead nurture (8-12 emails)
│   ├── Win-back? → Re-engagement (3-5 emails)
│   ├── Sales? → Sales sequence (5-7 emails)
│   └── Retention? → Customer success (ongoing)
├── What's the trigger?
│   ├── New signup → Welcome
│   ├── Downloaded content → Nurture
│   ├── Inactive 30+ days → Win-back
│   ├── Requested demo → Sales
│   └── Purchased → Customer success
```

## Platform Selection Decision Tree

```
Which social platform?
├── Who's your audience?
│   ├── B2B decision makers → LinkedIn
│   ├── Gen Z / Young millennials → TikTok
│   ├── Millennials → Instagram
│   ├── Broad / Local → Facebook
│   └── Tech / News → Twitter/X
├── What content type?
│   ├── Long-form video → YouTube
│   ├── Short-form video → TikTok, Reels
│   ├── Professional content → LinkedIn
│   ├── Visual / Lifestyle → Instagram
│   └── Community / Groups → Facebook
```

## Conversion Optimization Decision Tree

```
Low conversions?
├── Where in funnel?
│   ├── Landing page → page-cro
│   │   ├── Traffic but no clicks? → Headline + CTA
│   │   ├── Clicks but no form fills? → Form optimization
│   │   └── Form fills but no submit? → Reduce friction
│   ├── Signup flow → signup-flow-cro
│   │   ├── Starting but not finishing? → Simplify form
│   │   └── Not starting? → Value proposition
│   └── Post-signup → onboarding-cro
│       ├── Not activating? → Onboarding flow
│       └── Activating but not converting? → Upgrade triggers
```

## Pricing Decision Tree

```
Pricing strategy?
├── What's your product?
│   ├── SaaS / Subscription → Value-based pricing
│   ├── Marketplace → Transaction fees
│   ├── Service → Hourly / Project-based
│   └── Physical → Cost-plus or competitive
├── What's your market position?
│   ├── Premium / Enterprise → High price, high value
│   ├── Mid-market → Competitive pricing
│   └── Budget / SMB → Low price, high volume
├── Growth goal?
│   ├── Market share → Lower prices, freemium
│   ├── Revenue optimization → Value-based, tiers
│   └── Profit maximization → Premium, bundling
```

## Budget Allocation Decision Tree

```
Where to allocate budget?
├── What's the primary goal?
│   ├── Awareness → Paid social, video ads
│   ├── Consideration → Content, SEO, retargeting
│   ├── Conversion → Search ads, landing page optimization
│   └── Retention → Email, customer success
├── What's working?
│   ├── High-performing channel? → Double down
│   ├── Nothing working? → Test new channels
│   └── Mix working? → Optimize allocation
```

## Campaign Launch Decision Tree

```
Launch campaign?
├── What type?
│   ├── Product launch → launch-strategy
│   ├── Content campaign → content-strategy
│   ├── Paid campaign → paid-advertising
│   └── Email campaign → email-marketing
├── What timeline?
│   ├── Urgent (<1 week) → Paid + Email
│   ├── Short (1-4 weeks) → Multi-channel push
│   └── Long (1+ months) → Organic + Paid
├── What budget?
│   ├── None → Organic only
│   ├── Limited → Focus on 1-2 channels
│   └── Significant → Multi-channel approach
```

## Content Repurposing Decision Tree

```
Repurpose this content?
├── What's the source?
│   ├── Long-form video → Clips, quotes, transcript, blog
│   ├── Blog post → Social, email, video script
│   ├── Webinar → Blog, clips, quotes, slides
│   ├── Case study → Blog, social, sales deck
│   └── Podcast → Blog, clips, quotes, video
├── What platform?
│   ├── LinkedIn → Carousel, text post, article
│   ├── Instagram → Reel, carousel, story
│   ├── Twitter → Thread, quote cards
│   ├── TikTok → Short video, trending format
│   └── YouTube → Long-form, shorts
```

---

## How to Use Decision Trees

1. **Identify the situation**: What's the problem or goal?
2. **Follow the tree**: Answer each question
3. **Implement the recommendation**: Start with the suggested approach
4. **Measure results**: Track metrics
5. **Iterate**: Adjust based on data
